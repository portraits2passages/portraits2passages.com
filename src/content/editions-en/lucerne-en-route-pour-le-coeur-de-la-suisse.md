Portraits 2 Passages Lucerne takes you to Switzerland, and more precisely to the canton of Lucerne! This time it's the turn of Adèle, an Italian with a passion for volcanoes, and Caroline, a French outdoor-sports enthusiast, to set off from Lausanne. On the programme: discovering the region through local sports, traditional music and singing, nature reserves, iconic monuments and food.
