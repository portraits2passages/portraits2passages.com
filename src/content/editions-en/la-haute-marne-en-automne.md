Welcome to the very first edition of Portraits 2 Passages: an intense adventure full of discoveries and challenges! For this first episode, we chose to spotlight two captivating personalities: Barbara and Santiago, thirty-somethings who are as passionate as they are fascinating. Together they set out to conquer a little-known region full of surprises: Haute-Marne.

This department, unfairly nicknamed “empty”, hides unsuspected treasures. Birthplace of the Marne, the Seine and the Aube, a land of craft traditions, it offers a striking contrast between its vast state forests and its exceptional built heritage.

It was this blend of authenticity and mystery that led us to choose this destination to launch our adventure. Our pairs' mission was to rediscover these places not as tourists but as travellers of old, relying entirely on the welcome and advice of local people to move forward.

With picturesque villages, vast forests and unexpected encounters, their journey promised to be memorable.

For this edition:

- Duration: one weekend.
- Transport: hitchhiking only (no personal vehicles or organised public transport).
- Technology: none. No mobile phone, no GPS.
- Extra constraints: the rain, and the All Saints' Day weekend.

How do you find your way to Chaumont without satellites? How do you track down Voltaire without Google? Find out in the video.
